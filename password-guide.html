Password safety basics

A strong password is long, unique and difficult to guess. Reusing passwords across accounts can make the consequences of a breach much worse.

Use unique passwords

Use a different password for each important account. A password manager can help create and store unique passwords.

Prefer length and randomness

Long randomly generated passwords are generally difficult to guess. Avoid predictable patterns, personal details, common phrases or password reuse.

Enable multi-factor authentication

Where available, enable multi-factor authentication (MFA), preferably using an authenticator app, passkey or other supported secure method.

Use our generator

The DailyKit password generator creates a random string in your browser. Consider generating a new password on a trusted device, saving it directly into your password manager, and not sharing it. Do not use generated passwords on shared or compromised devices.

Keep recovery options secure

Protect recovery email accounts and phone numbers, keep recovery codes somewhere secure, and be alert to phishing messages.

This guide is general information, not a guarantee of account security.
