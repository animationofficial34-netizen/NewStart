About DailyKit

DailyKit is a collection of free, simple browser-based tools designed to make everyday digital tasks easier. Our goal is to provide clear, convenient utilities that work on desktop and mobile without requiring account registration.

Our approach

We aim to make useful tools accessible, fast and easy to understand. Tools on the site are provided for general informational and convenience purposes. Please independently verify important calculations and decisions.

What we offer

Our tools include text utilities, calculators, date helpers, unit conversions and other everyday utilities. We may improve, add or update tools over time.
