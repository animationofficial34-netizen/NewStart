About DailyKit

DailyKit is a simple collection of free online utilities designed to make everyday tasks easier. Use our text tools, calculators, date helpers and converters without installing an app or creating an account. We aim to keep the experience fast, accessible and easy to use on phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Frequently asked questions

Are these tools free?

Yes. All tools on this page are free to use.

Do I need to create an account?

No account or registration is required.

Is my text uploaded?

Text counter operations are performed locally in your browser. The page does not upload text to a server.

Can I use DailyKit on mobile?

Yes. The page layout adapts to mobile and desktop screens.
