How to calculate percentages

A percentage represents a part out of 100. Percentages are used for discounts, marks, comparisons, budgets and many everyday calculations.

Find a percentage of a number

Convert the percentage to a decimal by dividing it by 100, then multiply by the number.

Example: 15% of 200 = 0.15 × 200 = 30.

Find what percentage one number is of another

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 45 out of 60 is (45 ÷ 60) × 100 = 75%.

Calculate a discount

Calculate the discount amount using the percentage formula, then subtract that amount from the original price. A 10% discount on ₹1,000 is ₹100, so the discounted price is ₹900, before any additional taxes or fees.

Use the DailyKit calculator

On the DailyKit homepage, enter the percentage and number into the Percentage Calculator, then select Calculate. Check the inputs and result before relying on it.

Calculations do not account for complex tax rules, rounding requirements or financial conditions.
